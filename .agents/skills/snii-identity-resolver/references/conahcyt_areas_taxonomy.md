# Official SECIHTI / CONAHCYT Knowledge Areas Taxonomy

Structure of the 9 SNII evaluation committees (*comisiones dictaminadoras*):

---

1. **Area I (Physics, Mathematics, and Earth Sciences / *Físico-Matemáticas y Ciencias de la Tierra*):** Astronomy, Physics, Mathematics, Geophysics, Oceanography.
2. **Area II (Biology and Chemistry / *Biología y Química*):** Biochemistry, Genetics, Organic/Inorganic Chemistry, Ecology.
3. **Area III (Medicine and Health Sciences / *Medicina y Ciencias de la Salud*):** Epidemiology, Pharmacology, Biomedicine, Neurosciences, Public Health.
4. **Area IV (Behavioral Sciences and Education / *Ciencias de la Conducta y la Educación*):** Psychology, Pedagogy, Applied Linguistics.
5. **Area V (Humanities / *Humanidades*):** Philosophy, History, Literature, Arts, Archaeology.
6. **Area VI (Social Sciences / *Ciencias Sociales*):** Sociology, Economics, Political Science, Law, Demography.
7. **Area VII (Agricultural and Ecosystem Sciences / *Ciencias de la Agricultura y Ecosistemas*):** Agronomy, Veterinary Medicine, Forestry, Agricultural Biotechnology.
8. **Area VIII (Engineering and Technological Development / *Ingenierías y Desarrollo Tecnológico*):** Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Electrical, Mechanical, and Materials Engineering.
9. **Area IX (Interdisciplinary / *Interdisciplinaria*):** Complexity Sciences, Sustainability, Nanotechnology, Data Science.
