# APC Economic Model and Diamond Savings

The TlachIA ecosystem models the financial flow of scientific publishing to contrast expenditure transferred to oligopolistic commercial publishers against public Diamond Open Access infrastructure.

---

## 1. Base Financial Parameters

- **Base APC Rate:** Extracted directly from the OpenAlex database (`apc_paid.value_usd`).
- **Diamond Opportunity Cost:** \$1,800 USD per publication. Corresponds to the weighted median APC in hybrid/gold journals of major commercial publishers (Elsevier, Springer-Nature, Wiley).

---

## 2. Derived Economic Indicators

1. **Total Accrued Expenditure:** $\sum \text{APC}_{\text{USD}}$.
2. **Mean Expenditure per Document:** $\frac{\sum \text{APC}_{\text{USD}}}{N_{\text{total}}}$.
3. **Institutional Social Return:** Cumulative savings retained by public universities by sustaining Diamond university publishing platforms.
