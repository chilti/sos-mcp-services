# Semantic Manifold Expert (MLE Intrinsic Dimension & UMAP Topology)

> **Language Rule:** Respond and label outputs in the user's language (default to English for script plots/labels, or pass `--lang es` when the user interacts in Spanish).

This skill models the manifold geometry of scientometric data and semantic embeddings prior to or in parallel with training the Kohonen Self-Organizing Map (SOM).

## 1. Value Chain Integration (Workspace-First)

1. **Inputs from the Workspace:**
   - Multidimensional matrices `./matrices/indicadores_som.parquet` generated by `tlachia-metrics-engine` (with 35+ indicators).
   - Trained synaptic weight vectors in `./reports/som_state.json`.
   - 768-dimensional semantic embeddings (`embedding_nomic`) retrieved by `openalex-mexico-semantic-search`.

2. **Key Methodological Functions:**
   - **Local Intrinsic Dimension Estimation ($d_{MLE}$ at the 95th percentile):** Quantifies how many effective degrees of freedom govern the indicator manifold before projecting it onto the 2D SOM lattice.
   - **UMAP Projection Coupled with SOM (`run_umap=True` in `som_tools.py`):** Every training run executed via `som-methodological-expert` (`scripts/som_pipeline_cli.py`) computes and stores the 2D UMAP coordinates of the neural prototypes in `./reports/som_state.json`, enabling direct comparison between the topological preservation of the hexagonal lattice and the continuous UMAP manifold.
