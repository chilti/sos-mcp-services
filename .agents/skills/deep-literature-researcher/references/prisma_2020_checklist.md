# PRISMA 2020 Checklist (Adapted for Computational and Bibliometric Reviews)

Based on: Page, M. J., McKenzie, J. E., Bossuyt, P. M., Boutron, I., Hoffmann, T. C., Mulrow, C. D., ... & Moher, D. (2021). *The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews*. BMJ, 372, n71.

---

## 27-Item Structure for Systematic Review Reports

### Title and Abstract
1. **Title:** Explicitly identify the manuscript as a systematic review, scoping review, or systematic mapping study.
2. **Structured Abstract:** Provide background, objectives, data sources, eligibility criteria, synthesis methods, main results, limitations, and conclusions.

### Introduction
3. **Rationale:** Describe the theoretical rationale for the review in the context of existing knowledge and identified gaps.
4. **Objectives:** Explicit formulation of research questions based on the PICOS/PCC framework.

### Methods
5. **Eligibility Criteria:** Specify inclusion and exclusion criteria (population, phenomenon, time window, document type, language).
6. **Information Sources:** Detail all databases searched (ClickHouse, OpenAlex, WoS, Scopus, PubMed) with coverage dates and date of last access.
7. **Search Strategy:** Present the full search query syntax for at least one primary database, enabling exact replication (complying with PRESS 2015).
8. **Selection Process:** Describe the methods for screening titles/abstracts and full texts, including number of reviewers or automated agents and disagreement resolution.
9. **Data Collection Process:** Describe the variables extracted from each study and the use of standardized tools.
10. **Data Items:** Define all outcomes sought (metrics, algorithms, sample sizes, application domains).
11. **Study Risk of Bias Assessment:** Criteria for auditing the methodological rigor of included studies.
12. **Effect / Synthesis Measures:** Metrics used to compare or aggregate findings (FWCI, accuracy, error rates).
13. **Synthesis Methods:** Explain the procedure for tabulation, thematic categorization, or meta-analysis.

### Results
14. **Study Selection:** Present the quantitative flow using the PRISMA 2020 Flow Diagram (number of records identified, removed as duplicates, screened, assessed in full text, and included).
15. **Study Characteristics:** Present the Evidence Extraction Matrix (*Synthesis Table*).
16. **Risk of Bias in Studies:** Summary of the methodological quality of primary studies.
17. **Results of Individual Studies:** Summary of findings for each included study.
18. **Results of Syntheses:** Points of quantitative or qualitative consensus.
19. **Reporting Biases:** Assessment of potential bias toward positive results or English-language literature.
20. **Certainty of Evidence:** Evaluation of the overall strength of the body of evidence.

### Discussion
21. **Discussion of Findings:** Interpretation of results within the broader theoretical context.
22. **Limitations:** Limitations inherent to the data sources (index coverage, linguistic biases) and review methods.
23. **Practical and Policy Implications:** Recommendations for institutional decision-making or science policy.
24. **Implications for Future Research:** Unresolved gaps (*Research Gaps*).

### Other Information
25. **Registration and Protocol:** Indicate whether the protocol was pre-registered (PROSPERO, OSF) and where it is available.
26. **Support / Funding:** Sources of support and role of funders.
27. **Competing Interests:** Explicit declaration of competing interests or absence thereof.
