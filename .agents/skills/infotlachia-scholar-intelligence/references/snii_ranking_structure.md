# Structure of the National System of Researchers Registry (SNII)

Mexico's National System of Researchers (*Sistema Nacional de Investigadoras e Investigadores*, SNII) classifies scientists into official accreditation levels.

---

## 1. Accreditation Levels

* **Candidato a Investigador Nacional (National Researcher Candidate):** Early career stage (initial postdoctoral years), demonstrating independent research capability.
* **Nivel I (Level I):** Consistent scientific output of international impact and training of human resources (undergraduate/graduate theses).
* **Nivel II (Level II):** Consolidated research leadership, established lines of inquiry, and sustained citation recognition.
* **Nivel III (Level III):** Undisputed international leadership, formation of research groups, and pioneering scientific contributions.
* **Investigador Emérito (Emeritus Researcher):** Lifetime distinction awarded to scholars with decades of sustained excellence.

---

## 2. Knowledge Areas (I to IX)

* **Area I:** Physics, Mathematics, and Earth Sciences (*Ciencias Físico-Matemáticas y Ciencias de la Tierra*)
* **Area II:** Biology and Chemistry (*Biología y Química*)
* **Area III:** Medicine and Health Sciences (*Medicina y Ciencias de la Salud*)
* **Area IV:** Behavioral Sciences and Education (*Ciencias de la Conducta y la Educación*)
* **Area V:** Humanities (*Humanidades*)
* **Area VI:** Social Sciences (*Ciencias Sociales*)
* **Area VII:** Agricultural, Livestock, Forestry, and Ecosystem Sciences (*Ciencias de la Agricultura, Agropecuarias, Forestales y de Ecosistemas*)
* **Area VIII:** Engineering and Technological Development (*Ingenierías y Desarrollo Tecnológico*)
* **Area IX:** Interdisciplinary (*Interdisciplinaria*)
