# Geopolitical Collaboration Analyst

> **Language Rule:** Respond and label outputs in the user's language (default to English for script plots/labels, or pass `--lang es` when the user interacts in Spanish).

Quantitative module and analytical engine for geopolitical science mapping and international science diplomacy to analyze cross-border knowledge flows, evaluate strategic affinities, and diagnose national epistemological sovereignty.

---

## 1. Overview and Scope

Decoupled from external databases and optimized for standalone execution over any scientific corpus (Web of Science plain text `.txt`, `.csv`, `.parquet`, `.json`, or OpenAlex), this module addresses strategic science policy questions:
* **Who does a country or institution collaborate with?** Co-authorship matrices standardized in canonical [ISO-3166-1 alpha-3](references/iso_3166_alpha3_dictionary.md) codes.
* **What is the true bilateral affinity?** Normalization using **Salton's Cosine** and the **Jaccard Index**, mitigating the size bias that favors scientific superpowers.
* **Toward which geopolitical blocs does production gravitate?** Percentage quantification of cooperation toward **Ibero-America**, the **Global South**, the **OECD**, **BRICS**, and the **European Union**.
* **Who exercises intellectual leadership?** Diagnosis of epistemological sovereignty via first-author ($L_{\text{first}}$) and corresponding-author ($L_{\text{corr}}$) positions, distinguishing symmetrical consortia from asymmetrical or *"helicopter science"* schemes.

## 4. Methodological Foundations and Formulas

### 4.1 Bilateral Salton's Cosine
Normalizes co-authorship by accounting for the relative output capacities of both countries:

$$\text{Salton}_{ij} = \frac{C_{ij}}{\sqrt{T_i \times T_j}}$$

* $C_{ij}$: Jointly co-authored papers between anchor country $i$ and partner country $j$.
* $T_i, T_j$: Total individual output of country $i$ and country $j$ in the corpus.

### 4.2 Strategic Quadrant Matrix (Salton vs. Volume)
Categorizes partner countries into 4 quadrants relative to corpus medians:
1. **Core Strategic Partners (Quadrant I - High Salton / High Volume):** Consolidated, structural ties.
2. **Specialized Allies (Quadrant II - High Salton / Moderate Volume):** High relative affinity relationships that maximize thematic proximity over raw volume.
3. **Global Inertia Partners (Quadrant III - Low Salton / High Volume):** Scientific superpowers collaborated with due to their massive global critical mass, without necessarily reflecting preferential affinity.
4. **Peripheral Collaborations (Quadrant IV - Low Salton / Low Volume):** Sporadic contacts.

### 4.3 Leadership Rate and North-South Asymmetry

$$L_{\text{first}}(\text{anchor}) = \frac{N_{\text{first\_author\_anchor}}}{N_{\text{international\_papers}}} \times 100\%$$

| Leadership Level | Diagnosis | Science Policy Meaning |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| **$L \ge 50\%$** | **Sovereign / Preponderant** | The country formulates research questions, leads experiments, and drafts primary manuscripts. |
| **$40\% \le L < 50\%$** | **Symmetrical / Equitable** | Horizontal cooperation with a balanced distribution of responsibilities. |
| **$30\% \le L < 40\%$** | **Intermediate / Shared** | Active participation with frequent co-direction. |
| **$L < 30\%$** | **Asymmetrical / Subordinate** | *"Helicopter Science"*: the local country often acts as a provider of samples, fieldwork, or primary data, while intellectual direction resides in Global North centers. |

## 5. Anti-Patterns and Methodological Best Practices

| Anti-Pattern | Methodological Risk | Canonical Correction in the Skill |
|---|---|---|
| **Raw Co-authorship Count:** Reporting only $C_{ij}$ without normalization. | Size bias: the United States and China always top any ranking, masking strategic Latin American or Iberian affinities. | Always compute **Salton's Cosine** and the **Jaccard Index** to weight relative intensity. |
| **Blind 3-Character Truncation:** Truncating unknown strings to their first 3 characters. | Provinces, Chinese cities (Xiamen $\to$ `XIA`, Qingdao $\to$ `QIA`), or postal codes become false sovereign countries. | Strict use of the canonical ISO-3166-1 catalog (249 countries) and discarding strings not identified as a sovereign country. |
| **Confusing First Author with Corresponding Author:** Assuming they are always identical. | In many biomedical disciplines, the last author or corresponding author is the senior laboratory leader. | Independently extract $L_{\text{first}}$ (`C1` field / first block) and $L_{\text{corr}}$ (`RP` / Reprint author field). |
| **Ignoring Mega-Science and Multicenter Clinical Trials:** Failing to flag papers with >50 authors or high-energy physics. | In massive consortia (CERN, ATLAS, global clinical trials), the domestic first-author ratio can drop below 10% without implying extractive subordination. | Contextualize leadership indicators by filtering or flagging hyper-authorship papers. |
| **Mixing ISO-2 with ISO-3:** Using 2-letter and 3-letter codes interchangeably. | Inconsistency across geographic visualizers and tabular databases. | Automatic standardization to **ISO-3166-1 alpha-3** as the single canonical key. |
